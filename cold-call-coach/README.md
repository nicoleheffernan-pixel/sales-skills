# Cold Call Coach (Claude skill)

A Claude skill that helps SDRs, BDRs and AEs prepare for, practice and improve B2B cold calls, in English or Spanish (Spain).

Built by Nicole Heffernan with Claude, while preparing for sales interviews that included cold calling.

## What it does

- **Interview mock calls**: researches the hiring company, builds a one-screen call card, runs practice rounds and a coaching redo drill, and helps you answer "you've never cold called, why should we hire you?" honestly.
- **Real call prep**: one call card per prospect, plus a 15-second voicemail and a short follow-up email.
- **Role-play**: Claude plays a realistic, busy prospect at three difficulty levels, then scores you on 8 criteria and gives you the one thing to fix first.

Also included: an EN/ES objection bank, gatekeeper lines, a multi-touch cadence, sector packs (SaaS/AI, payments/fintech, marketplaces) and a Spain market module (tú vs usted, calling hours, the 2025 rules on commercial calls).

## Principles

- The goal of a cold call is the next meeting, not the sale.
- Beats you internalize, not a script you read.
- Honest openers only. No fake familiarity, no invented customers or stats.

## Install

- **Claude.ai**: Settings > Capabilities > Skills, upload the `cold-call-coach` folder (zipped).
- **Claude Code**: copy the folder into `~/.claude/skills/`.

Then try: "Let's practice a cold call. Play a Head of Operations at a 200-person logistics company."
